# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a safe, welcoming, and inclusive community for all participants in the Nexular Framework project. We value the participation of each member and want everyone to have a positive experience.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that creates a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping others learn and grow

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, religion, or technology choices
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the behaviors above
- Spam or off-topic content

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Report it** to the maintainers at `conduct@nexular-framework.dev`
2. **Include details** about the incident and context
3. **Be respectful** - we take all reports seriously

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Consequences

Violators of this Code of Conduct may face:

- Warnings or temporary restrictions
- Removal from the community
- Permanent bans from participation

Decisions will be made on a case-by-case basis.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull request discussions
- Discussion forums
- Project documentation
- Community chats
- Conferences and meetups
- Social media when representing the project
